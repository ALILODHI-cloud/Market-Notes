---

**Reason for entering trade:** in the event of a hawkish shock (sell-off across the curve), Mar-28 had more room to sell-off than Mar-27 — and hence would sell-off more (this is ultimately what came to pass). In the event of a dovish shock (rally across the curve), Mar-27 had more room to rally — and hence would rally more. Thus steepening screened as an asymmetric bet.

**What happened:** over the course of the trade's life, the curve bear steepened.

On the margin there were some macro reasons behind my conviction too. Much Barclays research suggesting that the neutral rate should be repriced higher, for instance. 5y5y sold-off over this horizon too. Did it move in tandem with the Mar-28 contract? The question of the extent to which 'neutral-rate-higher' arguments constituted good reasons to enter into Mar-27/Mar-28 steepener is something I can look at later on.

We now see opportunity in going short ECB vs BOE end-2026 pricing @ 4.5bps (ECB is priced to hike 24.5bps vs BOE at 29.0bps). Thesis to be discussed in the next post.
